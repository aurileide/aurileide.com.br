About me

My name is Aurileide Alves, but you can call me Auri. I love Design and work with digital products since 2000 and, with the education of Design undergraduate students, since 2012.

Actually, I am a Product Designer at Sicredi, working with other talented designers to keep improving the experience journey for our users. From February to May 2022, I was also the Product Designer Lead volunteer at Portal Lacrei, working at the professional journey squad.

Learning new things is what makes me get out of bed in the morning. So, I try to be opened minded and make room for new perspectives. Because of that, I went back to the Academy to get a Master's Degree in Information Design (2014-2016, UFPE) and a Ph.D. in Design (2017-2022, ULisboa).

As a designer, I am passionate about creating digital useful products for real people, always with a "little help" from my team.

I live in Fortaleza, Ceará and I love working remotely mostly because it is so easy to concentrate, which increases my productivity. Besides, I can still work from home, with my two cats Mafalda and Guile on my lap, or from anywhere in the world. Because I love traveling!

UI Design and Coding

I started my career in 2000 as a web designer, which allowed me to gain a great deal of experience building websites from scratch, prototyping using Fireworks, coding using HTML and CSS, and launching them to the WWW.

Prototyping

During my UI Design job, I had the opportunity to perfect my prototyping skills. Pencil and paper are the best tools ever, and I have seen many digital tools come and go. Today, my favorite tools are Illustrator, XD and Figma.

Research

Since 2012 I've been a design educator and researcher. This allowed me to gain a great deal of experience conducting in-depth interviews and exploring research methods and techniques with qualitative and quantitative aproaches.

Product Designer

For 14 years I helped to shape the design team, conducted the design process of the products and helped to deliver both digital and non digital solutions to solve real problems.

Design Professor

For almost ten years I`ve been an assistant professor for undergraduates of Design and Graphic Design. I am proud to see how many of them became successful professionals in the market. Sharing knowledge is one of the things that makes me happy in life.

Crochet, plants, cats and a backpack

When I'm not working, I love to crochet (like this blouse in the photo), draw, take care of plants, play with my cats and watch series. Traveling and discovering new cultures, meeting new people and ways of living are also habits I cultivate. I always have my backpack ready to go.
